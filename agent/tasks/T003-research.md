# T003 — Consolidate Competitive and Technical Research

## Goal
Turn the existing category discovery into a durable research dossier that defines what `dupe` should and should not compete on.

## Requirements
- Document dupeGuru as a baseline competitor.
- Compare representative duplicate-finder approaches at the level needed to define differentiation and fair benchmarking.
- Separate product features from technical implementation strategies.
- Identify the concrete J2-specific research questions.
- Record primary-source links and dates for important claims.

## Constraints
- Research, not feature cloning.
- Do not make performance claims without measured evidence.
- Preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

## Definition of done
`docs/RESEARCH.md` contains a concise competitive matrix, technical landscape, differentiation thesis, open questions, and sources sufficient for another agent to continue without chat history.
